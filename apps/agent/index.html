Blindspot · agent wallet

Let AI agents pay for you — without trusting them with your money.

AI agents can be tricked by a poisoned invoice, a fake website or a malicious token. Blindspot checks every payment against rules you set on your ENS name, privately, before anything is signed — and the chain enforces those rules even if the agent is fooled.

How it works
1

You set the rules

Who can be paid, how much per payment and per day, when to ask you. Stored on your ENS name — only you can change them.

2

The agent asks

The agent holds no keys and no money. It can only propose a payment to your Blindspot wallet.

3

Blindspot checks

Every payment is simulated and checked. Allowed, blocked, or sent to you for approval — with a plain reason and the exact source of a trick.

Why it's different

Enforced on-chain

Even a stolen agent key can't move money outside your rules: the smart contract refuses.

Private by default

Every read and payment goes through Anon-RPC, so no one links your activity to your IP address.

See where it went wrong

When something is blocked, you see which document or page tried to trick the agent.

You stay in control

Change limits in seconds, approve big payments, or disconnect the agent instantly.
